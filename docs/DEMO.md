# Demo script (~5 min) — rehearse on the LIVE url

1. **Insights → Brief.** Open `/insights` and show last week's cited report and cards. Open `/studio`: the same cards are pre-listed on the brief form. Select two.
2. **Bengali brief.** Paste a brief written in Bengali for a thriller launch. Generate (passcode). Point out the "Applied insights" section on the plan and how each card changed the plan.
3. **Tailoring.** On `/review`, show the 3 channels × 2 languages. Point to the three different image prompts and compositions, the X vs IG vs Shorts copy length, hashtag and CTA differences, and the dHash similarity numbers.
4. **Native Bengali.** Show the critic score and the independence check. Read the bn hook next to the en hook: different ideas, not a translation.
5. **Gate.** Try to schedule an unapproved variant and show that it's blocked. Discard one with a note, regenerate, and show version 2. Approve the rest, then edit one to show its approval is cleared.
6. **Publish.** Schedule, press ⏩ advance clock, and show published posts with external IDs.
7. **Rule-breaker.** Submit a 1:1 video to Shorts, an 11 MB image to IG and a 310-char X post. Show that all three are rejected with reasons, plus the attempt log.
8. **Analytics.** Advance the clock, metrics come in, open the like-for-like comparison for this concept.
9. **Report.** Generate the weekly report. Every sentence has post chips; click one to jump to the post. New insight cards appear in the brief form, and the loop is closed.
